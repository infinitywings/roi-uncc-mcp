# IEEE-123 GridLAB-D Cadence Experiment

Date: 2026-07-28  
Status: **component repair validated for 10-second actuation; 10-second
closed-loop feedback not achieved**

## Experiment

The actual GridEval Feeder A model was run in the pinned `docker-cosim`
environment with GridLAB-D 5.3.0 and HELICS 3.6.1. The source model was
mounted read-only. Each arm used a generated temporary overlay and an
isolated, fixed source-model swing voltage; GridPACK was deliberately excluded
so distribution-side cadence and the EV4 command path could be measured
without transmission-side confounding.

Two arms received the same deterministic EV4 pulse:

- At simulation second 60: 200 kW → 400 kW.
- At simulation second 120: 400 kW → 200 kW.

| Arm | GridLAB-D HELICS period | Minimum timestep | Controller period |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen | 60 s | 60 s | 60 s |
| Physical-10 | 10 s | 10 s | 10 s |

The bounded 400 kW command is a diagnostic pulse, not an attacker maximum.

## Results

| Measurement | Frozen arm | Physical-10 arm |
|---|---:|---:|
| Controller grant/observation events | 4 | 24 |
| High command internally applied | t=120 | t=70 |
| High internal actuation latency | 60 s | 10 s |
| High effect visible to controller | t=180 | t=80 |
| High controller-visible latency | 120 s | 20 s |
| Restore internally applied | t=180 | t=130 |
| Restore internal actuation latency | 60 s | 10 s |
| Restore visible to controller | t=240 | t=140 |
| Restore controller-visible latency | 120 s | 20 s |
| GridLAB-D exit | Success | Success |
| Error/fatal diagnostics | 0 | 0 |

Before the pulse at t=60, the arms differed by only 0.30 W total and less
than 1 W on every phase, versus approximately 3.70 MW feeder power. This
passes the frozen 1 W component-equivalence tolerance.

After correcting phase-C changes for the mean contemporaneous drift on phases
A and B:

- Frozen high effect: +227.372 kW.
- Physical-10 high effect: +225.613 kW.
- Difference: −1.760 kW, or 0.88% of the commanded 200 kW step.
- Frozen restore effect: −225.240 kW.
- Physical-10 restore effect: −225.253 kW.
- Difference: −0.013 kW, or 0.006% of the commanded step.

Thus the bounded physical effect magnitude is equivalent within the
prespecified 2% tolerance, and the v3 overlay reduces internal actuation
latency from 60 to 10 seconds.

## Interpretation

The experiment separates two events that the manuscript currently conflates:

1. **Plant actuation:** GridLAB-D records EV4 changing one federate period
   after command send—60 seconds in the frozen arm and 10 seconds in the
   repaired arm.
2. **Closed-loop observation:** the controller receives the feeder-power
   publication one additional period later—120 seconds in the frozen arm and
   20 seconds in the repaired arm.

The v3 physical-10 overlay therefore validates a 10-second *actuation*
cadence, but not a fresh 10-second observation/response cycle under
non-iterative HELICS coupling. The frozen v2 configuration supports neither:
its actuation and feedback latencies are 60 and 120 seconds.

Consequently:

- The manuscript's claimed frozen 10-second physical defender mechanism is
  falsified.
- V3 may use the physical-10 overlay if its protocol distinguishes 10-second
  command actuation from 20-second closed-loop feedback.
- A true 10-second observation-to-observation loop would require HELICS
  iterative coupling or another synchronization redesign and a new
  equivalence test.

## Preserved failures

Two failed revisions are retained rather than erased:

1. The first launch failed before simulation because recorder outputs were
   symlinked into the read-only source mount. It generated zero controller
   events and is classified as probe-infrastructure failure.
2. The first physical pulse used the campaign maximum of 1.5 MW. GridLAB-D
   reached the FBS convergence iteration limit at t=120. This is a
   physical-model failure, not missing data. The bounded 400 kW diagnostic
   pulse was then chosen to isolate cadence.

The 1.5 MW failure means the isolated fixed-voltage component model cannot
support campaign-scale attack commands without the complete coupled
federation or additional solver/model work.

## Reproducibility and scope

- Both successful arms were rerun from fresh temporary overlays.
- Each repeat was byte-identical to its primary JSON trace.
- The analysis was independently regenerated byte-for-byte.
- The source v2 and IEEE-123 files were not modified.
- This component experiment does not validate GridPACK coupling, attacker
  inference, NATIG, OpenDER, or a full campaign.
